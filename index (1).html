Current web tools

Useful before, during, and after every war.

Leaders plan once, and the whole leadership team sees the same plan on every device. Members see their own orders.

Village Tracker

Hero, equipment, pet, and lab levels load straight from your verified game account. Paste your village data in one tap to add buildings, walls, and upgrade timers.

Upgrade Planner

Build a queue, assign builder or laboratory lanes, estimate start and completion dates, and account for manual time and cost discounts.

War Room

Live war data from the game, shared target assignments for both attacks, and an automatically ordered cleanup board — the same for every leader.

CWL Bonus Tracker

Track seven daily scores, average destruction, ranking, and bonus positions in one shared season, then archive it as a read-only record.

Clan Messages

Generate assignments, cleanup lists, and reminders formatted into messages that respect the in-game character limit.

Roles from the game

Your in-game leader owns the workspace, co-leaders manage it, and members follow along. Promotions, demotions, and departures update automatically.
